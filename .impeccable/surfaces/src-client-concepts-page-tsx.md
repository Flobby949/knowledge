Scope and mode: Concept workspace route; organize and maintain.

Audience and job: a single user turning several verified knowledge points into one durable, self-contained definition while preserving exact sources and change history.

Primary task and proof: the definition and current Memberships lead. Every member names its source record and current/review state; revision and activity history remain available without displacing the working set.

Chosen direction: a compact concept catalog beside one assembly sheet, borrowing the density and direct addressability of a printed catalog while staying inside the established dual-theme knowledge workbench. Catalog codes and Membership codes are navigation coordinates, not domain identifiers.

Important state: creation requires a name, description, and at least two current knowledge points. Archived Concepts remain readable; stale Memberships stay attached and visibly require review rather than silently migrating.

Constraints: preserve theme switching, source links, stable Concept and Membership identity, immutable revisions, reversible removal and archive actions, format-4 restore compatibility, keyboard access, and 1280px desktop geometry.
